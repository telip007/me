# Expressive identity research

Checked 29 October 2026. This research replaces the earlier cool-white/cobalt visual recommendation. Scope: a distinctive personal publication about technology, coding and AI broadly. The task is to develop a coherent graphic identity for writing, not to assemble fashionable components. The directions below are design proposals, not claims about a universal trend or measured reader preference.

### How a distinct system is developed

1. **Name the editorial relationship first.** This publication makes technical ideas inspectable through explanations, code, examples and sources. Pick a concrete visual relationship from that process; let it generate the identity. A slogan alone cannot do this.
2. **Choose one strong formal rule.** Examples: a controlled split, one expanding interval, or a recurring cut in an otherwise solid shape. Apply it to type placement, graphic detail and a single interaction. It should still be recognizable with the color removed.
3. **Give type actual roles.** Display expresses personality through width, weight and line composition. Reading text expresses care through optical quality and rhythm. Navigation expresses utility. A font pairing is not the whole identity.
4. **Use color as a family of relationships.** Decide which field carries the opening, which carries sustained reading, what distinguishes an action, and how diagrams can expand the palette. Do not make every section a different mood board.
5. **Connect shapes with behavior.** If the defining graphic is a split plane, a disclosure can separate along that seam; if it is an interval, focus can open that interval. Avoid unrelated pills, blobs, boxes and shadows.
6. **Keep brand expression and reading utility in different intensities.** The opening can be unmistakable; the article body can become quiet while retaining the same type, color and annotation grammar. This is one system with controlled amplitude.
7. **Test recognition, not decoration count.** Crop away the name: could the masthead, a source note and the footer still belong to the same publication? Change a German title into a longer English title: does the visual rule remain intact?

### Three grounded directions

These are deliberately different systems, not three palettes applied to one layout. None needs placeholder articles, content cards, cultural motifs or public explanation of personal background.

**1. The argument in layers — wine-black, chalk and sharp citron.**

Mechanism: two precisely offset planes, representing a technical statement and the detail that makes it inspectable. A heavy, slightly narrow sans title sits on a near-black wine field; a small exposed citron edge appears where the title plane meets the reading plane. The edge becomes a section marker, code annotation and a source-note connector. Article rows remain open typography. A single hover reveals the underlying rule by a few pixels; no permanent 3D scene. Article mode switches to chalk with dark wine ink, retaining the offset margin grammar. Warmth comes from depth of color and typography, not terracotta or decorative texture. Risk: turning every section into a layered box; constrain the mechanism to the opening, major transitions and annotations.

**2. The expanding index — graphite, butter-yellow and violet.**

Mechanism: one variable interval, like an index opening into an explanation. A huge broad/narrow typographic contrast makes the masthead feel almost poster-like; a short horizontal interval expands into the live article sequence. Butter-yellow is a confident field rather than a tiny button accent, with graphite reading text and a limited violet signal. A topic/focus change expands the relevant interval; it never scatters the list into an unconventional navigation puzzle. Body reading uses a pale warm field and a restrained sans text face. This direction borrows Material's willingness to use scale and tension, not its pill components. Risk: typography becoming a motion demo; retain a composed still frame and optional movement.

**3. The margin instrument — deep forest, silver-gray and warm white.**

Mechanism: a continuous margin line that changes thickness to connect a question, code sample and source. The opening uses an asymmetric composition with a large cut in the forest field; the cut defines a reading window rather than a card. A characterful sans masthead and exceptionally clear text face keep it technical without console styling. Small silver annotations belong to the same line system. The line resolves into normal section rules on mobile and remains entirely outside prose. A deliberate selection can illuminate its corresponding segment; scrolling itself stays untouched. Risk: becoming a dashboard or fabricated measurement display; use no numbers, ticks or labels unless they explain real content.

### Recommendation

Develop **the argument in layers** first. Its formal tension can make an empty index feel authored without manufacturing content, supports a dark expressive opening and calm light reading, and offers a concrete graphic rule that can carry through source notes and article structure. Make one complete opening and one real-text article treatment before calling the direction resolved. If those two surfaces require unrelated visual tricks, the identity has not yet become a system.
