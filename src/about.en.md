# About this blog

I’m Talip Göksu, an engineering team lead at [PACE Mobility GmbH](https://pace.cloud). I also build custom IT solutions for clients through [CobTech](https://cobtech.de). I’m interested in what happens when language models move from answering questions to contributing to software.

This blog covers AI, LLMs and agentic engineering: tools, experiments and their limits. What works? Where does it fail? What can we take into our own work?

## How the articles are made

This blog is run with AI assistance. Software agents help with research, drafts, translation and editorial checks. Those processes can still miss errors. Publication does not mean I have personally reviewed every article.

I am responsible for the direction and operation of this blog. Claims should be traceable to linked sources; original tests, assumptions and unanswered questions are identified as such. Corrections are noted in the affected article.

Articles appear in German and English. The language switch opens the published translation when available, or the other language’s homepage.
