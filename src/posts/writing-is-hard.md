I started this blog because I had so many ideas I thought would be cool to write about. Turns out, having ideas was the easy part.

Sometimes I write a few sentences and feel like I’ve already said everything. Now what? Other times, I can’t even get the thought onto the page in a way that makes sense. Somewhere between having an idea and turning it into a post, I get stuck.

Finding a structure, figuring out what needs explaining, adding something without just repeating myself: that’s a lot harder than I expected. So, with yet another unfinished draft, I figured I’d write about that instead.

## I wanted the practice

Being able to explain an idea clearly is a skill I really value. A blog seemed like a good way to get better at it. It also gives me a reason to dig deeper into topics and, hopefully, connect with people who have something to add or a different way of looking at them.

I still like that idea. I’m just finding out how much work it  really takes behind the scenes.

## The pressure to be faster

I use AI a lot, for coding and for writing these posts. It helps me catch mistakes, untangle sentences, and find words when I’m stuck. I’m happy to have that help.

But it also makes me more impatient. When a tool can produce a whole bunch of posts in the blink of an eye, struggling with a paragraph starts to feel ridiculous. Add the impression that everyone else is suddenly doing ten things at once, and taking time to learn feels harder to justify.

I could hand over a topic and get a post back. But I started this blog partly to learn how to develop and communicate my own ideas. I want AI to help me work through that, and I still need to be involved in figuring out what I’m trying to say.

## Still figuring it out

I’m a beginner. Of course the posts I start don’t come out the way I want them to yet. Apparently, knowing that doesn’t stop me from getting frustrated about it.

I don’t have a neat lesson to end on. I mostly needed to get this off my chest. Writing it down has made me a little more willing to go back to the other drafts, though. That’s something.
